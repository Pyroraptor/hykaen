* In scope (v0) vs Deferred (out of v0 scope)

** In scope — the v0 combat slice

  - The seven attributes and 27-point point-buy.
  - All derived stats: HP, Stamina, the four defenses, Initiative, Speed,
    Skills Known.
  - The seven-action *round* economy, turn structure, the free Step, the
    Spent state, and Combinations.
  - Passive defenses (AC, Reflex, Fortitude, Will) and the four active-defense
    reactions (Dodge, Block, Parry, Steel), plus Opportunistic Strike and the
    Shield spell-reaction.
  - The full v0 action/reaction card set (this document, §Actions & reactions).
  - Critical hits and Tier-based =[W]= damage scaling (Tier 1 is the only tier
    actually used by the vignette; Tier 2/3 multipliers are defined for the
    data model but no v0 content reaches them).
  - The minimal condition set: *Spent, Off-Guard, Dazed, Immobilized, Haste,
    Stunned*.
  - Two spellcasting paradigms used by the party: *Channeling* (Divine
    Channeler, Divine Warrior) and *Prepared/Attuned* (Wizard).
  - The v0 spell set (the five source spells: Channel, Shield, Fire Bolt,
    Heal, Fireball) and the v0 weapon & armor tables.
  - The four playable classes (Fighter, Wizard, Divine Warrior, Divine
    Channeler) with 1–2 authored talent tiers each, including a stealth talent
    on Marigold's Divine Champion path.
  - A minimal v0 skill list (combat needs Mobility and Determination; the rest
    support exploration/social checks).

  - *The Sorcerer Stamina-to-energy paradigm* and any third paradigm beyond

  - *Conditions beyond the five named above* (Prone, Frightened, Poisoned,
    Grappled, Blinded, etc.).
  - *The other four classes*: Rogue, Sorcerer, Primal Channeler, Primal
    Warrior — and all their unique trees (Lethality, Poisons, Primal *, …).
  - *Full talent-tree contents and full spell lists.* v0 ships only the

  - *Racial mechanics.* Human / Tiefling / Elf / Dwarf are cosmetic; Race HP

  - *Tier 2/3 character scaling, multi-level progression, and leveling past
    Level 1.* Vignette is Tier 1 / Level 1 throughout.
  - *Enemy AI sophistication.* v0 enemy AI is "select a legal action

  - *Skills as a ranked/trained subsystem.* v0 skills are flat

* The seven attributes & point-buy

  Source: =Building a Character.pdf=.

  | Attribute    | Governs (combat-relevant)                                      |
  |--------------+----------------------------------------------------------------|
  | Strength     | Melee attack (Str weapons), Fortitude                          |
  | Constitution | HP, Stamina, Fortitude                                         |
  | Dexterity    | Ranged & finesse attack, Reflex, Initiative, AC (light/none)   |
  | Willpower    | Will defense, Stamina, Determination (Steel)                   |
  | Intellect    | Skills Known count, Wizard spellcasting                        |
  | Intuition    | Initiative, Reflex                                             |
  | Social       | Will defense, Divine Channeler spellcasting                    |

  *Point-buy:* every attribute starts at 0. Distribute *27 points*. A score's
  cost is cumulative per the table:

  | Score | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |  6 |
  |-------+---+---+---+---+---+---+----|
  | Cost  | 0 | 1 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 9 | 12 |

  The allocator must reject any build whose total cost exceeds 27 and must
  reject scores outside 0–6 in v0.

  *No 5e terminology.* Charisma, Wisdom, Intelligence (as an attribute name),
  proficiency bonus, bonus actions, cantrips, and ASIs do not appear in this
  system. (Weapon "Prof." in the weapon table is a per-weapon attack bonus,
  not a level-scaling proficiency bonus.)

* Derived stats

  All formulas below are fixed for v0 (Tier = 1, Level = 1, Race HP = 8).

  - *Hit Points* — =HP = 8 + Class HP + Constitution=.
    (General form, deferred: =Race HP + Class HP × Level + Constitution × Tier=.)
  - *Stamina* — =Stamina = Class Stamina base + Willpower + Constitution=.
    Stamina is a shared pool spent on Combining actions (1 each) and on
    healing (the Heal spell trades the target's Stamina for HP). It refills
    fully only on an uninterrupted long rest.
  - *Defenses* (each is a passive target number):
    - *Armor Class (AC)* = =10 + armor base + Dexterity=, where the Dexterity
      term applies *only* if the worn armor is category Light or None.
    - *Reflex* = =10 + Dexterity + Intuition=.
    - *Fortitude* = =10 + Constitution + Strength=.
    - *Will* = =10 + Willpower + Social=.
  - *Initiative* — =d20 + Intuition + Dexterity=, rolled once at combat start;
    sets turn order for the whole combat. (Confirmed by the Bobbin/Dmitri
    worked example.)
  - *Speed* — *20 ft = 4 squares*. One square = 5 ft. A Step moves 1 square; a Stride moves up
    to Speed.
  - *Skills Known* — =Class Skills base + Intellect= (the count of skills the
    character is trained in; see §Skills).

* Skills (v0)

  The source =Skills= section is an empty stub. For v0, a skill check is
  =d20 + governing attribute= against a DC (no separate skill ranks). "Skills
  Known" counts which skills a character is *trained* in; in v0 a trained
  skill simply grants a +2 to that check. The
  two combat-critical skills are *Mobility* and *Determination*.

  | Skill         | Attribute   | Used by                                  |
  |---------------+-------------+------------------------------------------|
  | Mobility      | Dexterity   | *Dodge* reaction (combat); movement      |
  | Determination | Willpower   | *Steel* reaction (combat); resist effects|
  | Perception    | Intuition   | exploration: spot/notice                 |
  | Stealth       | Dexterity   | exploration: hide; Marigold skirmisher   |
  | Thievery      | Dexterity   | exploration: locks/traps                 |
  | Athletics     | Strength    | exploration: climb/jump/shove            |
  | Persuasion    | Social      | dialogue checks                          |
  | Lore          | Intellect   | knowledge/investigation                  |

  Only *Mobility* and *Determination* are required for the v0 combat engine;
  the rest exist for the later exploration/dialogue phases and may be trimmed
  by the author. A fuller, ranked skill system is deferred.

* The round & action economy

  Source: =Combat.pdf=. *This section corrects the earlier per-turn
  misreading recorded in CR-001 / memory =hollow-world-action-economy=.*

  - A *round* is ~6 seconds. Each combatant receives *seven actions at the
    start of each round* — this is a *per-round* budget, not a per-turn one.
  - *Initiative* is rolled once at combat start and fixes turn order for the
    whole combat.
  - Within a round, combatants take *turns* in initiative order, and the order
    *cycles repeatedly*. On each of their turns a combatant may do two things:
    1. optionally take a free *Step* (move 5 ft / 1 square, triggers no
       reactions), and
    2. take *exactly one action*, which costs 1 from their 7-action round
       budget.
    If they take the free Step, that turn's action *may not have the Movement
    trait*.
  - Because each turn spends one action, a combatant gets *up to seven turns
    per round* (fewer if they spend actions on reactions).
  - *Reactions* are taken out of turn, in response to a trigger on someone
    else's turn. Each reaction *spends one action from the same 7-action
    round budget*. A combatant cannot react if they have no actions left.
  - A combatant with *0 actions remaining is Spent*: they take no further
    turns, actions, or reactions this round.
  - The *round ends* when every combatant is Spent (no participant has actions
    remaining). At round end: each combatant's budget resets to 7 (as modified
    by Haste/Stunned for the new round), all per-round/per-turn flags clear,
    the Guard bonus expires, and Channeling energy pools empty.

  *Worked illustration of the budget:* a Hasted Fighter (7 + 2 = 9 actions)
  who is forced to Block twice (−2) has 7 actions left to spend across their
  turns that round.

* Actions & reactions (v0 card set)

  Source: =Combat.pdf=. All have Stamina cost 0 unless combined (see
  §Combinations). The full action/reaction lists are:

  | Actions      | Reactions            |
  |--------------+----------------------|
  | Strike       | Block                |
  | Stride       | Parry                |
  | Cast a spell | Dodge                |
  | Interact     | Opportunistic Strike |
  | Guard        | Shield (a spell)     |
  | Take Cover   | Steel                |
  | Step (free)  |                      |

** Actions

  - *Strike* — 1 action. Traits: Repeat. Make a weapon attack: =d20 + (Str or
    Dex) + weapon Prof= vs the target's *Armor Class*, using the weapon's
    range and damage type. Hit: =1[W] + Str or Dex= damage (Tier scaling:
    2[W] at T2, 3[W] at T3).
  - *Stride* — 1 action. Traits: Movement, Repeat. Move up to your Speed.
  - *Cast a spell* — action cost per the spell. See §Spellcasting.
  - *Interact* — 1 action. Manipulate an object/environment.
  - *Guard* — 1 action. Traits: Repeat. Gain *+1 to all defenses* for the rest
    of the round.
  - *Take Cover* — 1 action. Gain a defensive benefit from cover (v0: +2 to AC
    and Reflex while you remain in cover; authored flat value, tunable).
  - *Step* — *free, Special*. Traits: Movement. Move 5 ft (1 square),
    triggering no reactions. Taking the free Step forbids your action this turn
    from having the Movement trait.

** Reactions (each spends 1 action from the round budget)

  - *Block* — Trigger: you are *hit* by an attack vs Armor Class. Reduce
    damage by =1[W] + your weapon's Block value= (2[W]/3[W] at T2/T3). On-hit
    side effects are negated. If your blocking weapon has a Block die, use it
    in place of the weapon die.
  - *Parry* — Trigger: you are *hit* by an attack vs Armor Class. Make an
    attack roll + your weapon's Parry bonus; if it exceeds the attacker's
    attack roll, the attack deals 0 damage and on-hit effects are negated.
  - *Dodge* — Trigger: you are *targeted* by an attack vs Armor Class or
    Reflex and are not Immobilized. Make a *Mobility check*; use its result in
    place of your AC/Reflex against that attack, then Step. If there is no open
    square to Step into, the Mobility check automatically fails to protect you.
  - *Opportunistic Strike* — Trigger: an enemy provokes (v0: an adjacent enemy
    uses a Movement- or Concentrate-trait action — i.e. Strides or casts most spells — out of your reach without
    Stepping). Make one Strike against it. (v0 trigger is authored; flag if you
    want a different provocation rule.)
  - *Steel* — Trigger: you are *targeted* by an attack vs Fortitude or Will and
    are conscious. Make a *Determination check*; use its result in place of
    your Fortitude/Will. If you resist, gain +2 to your next check this round.
  - *Shield* (spell-reaction) — see §Spellcasting; Trigger: hit by an attack vs
    AC or Reflex. You Block using die 1d6 and Block value +4.

* Combinations

  Source: =Combat.pdf=. Spend *1 Stamina* to *Combine* two actions into a
  single action (you still take only one action that turn, but it does more):

  - Combine two *different* actions — e.g. *Charge* = Stride + Strike;
    *Riposte* = (Block or Parry) + Strike.
  - Combine an action with *itself* only if it has the *Repeat* trait — e.g.
    sprint = Stride + Stride; double-attack = Strike + Strike.
  - Reactions may be combined with actions (the Riposte case).
  - The *Dazed* condition forbids all combining.

  Each Combine costs 1 Stamina and consumes *one* action slot (not two).

* Attack & defense resolution pipeline

  The resolution module takes an attack and returns a structured result
  (=hit=, =crit=, =damage_raw=, =active_defense_applied=, =damage_final=).
  Order of resolution:

  1. *Determine attack total.*
     - Weapon attack: =d20 + (Str or Dex) + weapon Prof bonus=.
     - Spell attack: =d20 + spellcasting attribute + spell-attack bonus=
       (v0 spell-attack bonus = *+0*; see §Authored v0 Decisions). Spell
       targets the defense named by the spell (e.g. Fire Bolt vs Reflex).
  2. *Apply the defender's active defense, if any* (each spends 1 action):
     - *Dodge* (vs AC/Reflex attacks, not Immobilized): replace the targeted
       defense with the Mobility-check result for this attack.
     - *Steel* (vs Fortitude/Will attacks, conscious): replace the targeted
       defense with the Determination-check result.
     - *Parry* (vs AC attacks, on a hit): if defender's (attack roll + Parry
       bonus) > attacker's attack total, the attack deals 0 and is negated.
  3. *Compare.* Hit if attack total ≥ target defense (after substitution).
  4. *Critical hit* if the attack total *exceeds the defense by 10 or more*:
     roll damage dice twice and double the flat modifier. (Talents may lower
     this threshold or add dice — none in the v0 set do.)
  5. *Compute damage* (see §Damage) and apply *Block* if used (vs AC hits):
     subtract =1[W] + Block value= (or the Block die).
  6. Return the structured result.

  Passive defenses (AC/Reflex/Fortitude/Will) are always-on target numbers;
  the four active defenses (Dodge/Block/Parry/Steel) are opt-in reactions that
  substitute a check or reduce/negate damage. This passive+active duality is
  the system's defining tactical hook and must be preserved, not simplified.

* Damage & Tier scaling

  - Base weapon damage = =1[W] + modifier= where =[W]= is the weapon die and
    the modifier is Str or Dex (weapon attacks) or the spellcasting attribute
    (where a spell adds it).
  - *Tier scaling* multiplies the weapon dice: =1[W]= → =2[W]= (Tier 2) →
    =3[W]= (Tier 3). Tier is a data-driven parameter. *The vignette is Tier 1
    throughout*; the 2[W]/3[W] multipliers are defined for the data model but
    no v0 content uses them.
  - On a *critical hit*, dice are rolled twice and the flat modifier is
    doubled.

* Conditions (v0 set)

  Only these six exist in v0. Each has apply / remove / query semantics.
  Applying an already-active condition does not stack unless stated.

  | Condition   | Effect                                                                          |
  |-------------+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
  | Spent       | 0 actions remaining; cannot take actions or reactions this round. (A state, derived from the action budget, tracked here for query symmetry.)               |
  | Off-Guard   | Attacks against you gain a +2 bonus. |
  | Dazed       | Cannot Combine any actions. Off-Guard to all attacks.                           |
  | Immobilized | Speed 0; cannot take Movement-trait actions; cannot use the Dodge reaction.      |
  | Haste X     | Round action budget *+X*.            |
  | Stunned X   | Round action budget *−X*, to a minimum of 0. Off-Guard to all attacks. |

  Haste/Stunned magnitudes are authored starting values — the simulation
  harness exists precisely to tune them. (FR-005 wires Dazed → action economy's
  combine gate, and Immobilized → resolution's Dodge gate.)

* Spellcasting (v0 — two paradigms)

  Source: =Magic and Spellcasting.pdf=. Only the two paradigms the party uses
  are in scope; the Sorcerer Stamina-to-energy paradigm is *deferred*.

** Channeling — Divine Channeler & Divine Warrior

  A per-round energy pool. The *Channel* action adds 1 energy to the pool
  (max capacity = the caster's spellcasting attribute score). Energy is spent
  to cast known spells and the *pool empties at end of round*. Casting is
  rejected if the pool has insufficient energy.
  - Divine Channeler spellcasting attribute = *Social* (pool max = Social).
  - Divine Warrior spellcasting attribute = *Willpower* (pool max = Willpower).

** Prepared/Attuned — Wizard

  Each day the Wizard prepares known spells into attuned items: count of
  prepared spells = number of attuned items; *total prepared energy ≤ level*.
  Casting a spell of energy cost > 0 *discharges* that item until it is
  re-primed; *0-energy spells do not discharge*. Wizards may *ritual-cast*
  eligible spells out of combat. Wizard spellcasting attribute = *Intellect*.
  - *v0/Level-1 consequence:* prepared energy budget ≤ 1, so a Level-1 Wizard
    effectively fields 0-energy spells (Fire Bolt, Shield) plus at most 1
    energy of prepared spell. *Fireball (min energy 2) is therefore NOT
    castable at Tier 1 / Level 1* — it is included in the spell set for
    completeness and forward scaling only.

** v0 spell set

  Exactly these five (from the source). Action/energy/components per the cards:

  - *Channel* — 1 action, 0 energy, Concentrate. +1 to your Channeling pool
    (until end of round).
  - *Shield* — 1 reaction, 0 energy, Concentrate, 1 hand, Somatic. Trigger: hit
    by an attack vs AC or Reflex. You Block using die 1d6, Block value +4.
  - *Fire Bolt* — 2 actions, 0 energy, Concentrate, 1 hand, Somatic/Implement/
    Fire/Verbal. Ranged spell attack vs *Reflex*, range 50 ft; hit:
    =2d8 + spellcasting attribute= fire (4d8/6d8 at T2/T3).
  - *Heal* — variable actions, variable energy, Concentrate, 2-hand, Somatic/
    Verbal/Life. Per action spent, pick a target within 30 ft; per energy
    spent, the target may spend 1 Stamina to regain HP; if within reach, touch
    to add 1d6 per energy spent.
  - *Fireball* — 2 actions, variable energy (min 2), Concentrate, 2-hand,
    Somatic/Fire/Implement/Material/Verbal. 20-ft-radius sphere within 100 ft;
    area spell attack vs *Reflex*; =3d6 per energy + spellcasting attribute=
    fire, half on a miss, none on a critical miss. *(Not castable in v0 — see
    above.)*

  Additional spells and full lists are deferred to author authoring (Phase 3).

** Talent trees (1–2 authored tiers each — AUTHORED CONTENT)

  The source lists tree *names* but no talent *contents*. The following are
  authored v0 starters (1–2 tiers per class) — explicitly subject to author
  editing. A tree's Tier-2 talent requires the Tier-1 talent first.

  - *Fighter* — trees incl. Guardian, Weapon Training, Tactics.
    - Guardian T1 *Bulwark*: when you Block, add +1 to your weapon's Block
      value.
    - Guardian T2 *Interpose*: you may Block an attack that targets an ally in
      an adjacent square (still costs your reaction/action).
    - Weapon Training T1 *Weapon Focus*: +1 damage with one chosen weapon type.
  - *Wizard* — trees incl. Combat Magic, Metamagic, Ritual Casting.
    - Combat Magic T1 *Evoker's Precision*: +1 to spell attack rolls vs Reflex.
    - Combat Magic T2 *Penetrating Bolt*: Fire Bolt ignores 2 points of cover
      bonus.
    - Ritual Casting T1 *Ritualist*: prepare one extra 0-energy spell.
  - *Divine Warrior* — trees incl. Divine Champion (Marigold), Zeal, Weapon
    Styles.
    - *Divine Champion* T1 *Shadow of the Divine* (the required stealth
      talent): you are trained in Stealth and Mobility; while Stealthed you may
      Stride at full Speed without breaking Stealth once per round.
    - *Divine Champion* T2 *Sanctified Ambush*: your first Strike against a

* Worked example — Bobbin vs Dmitri (acceptance reproduction)

  Reproduced from =Combat.pdf= as a deterministic acceptance test for the
  resolution + initiative + Dodge pipeline (FR-004 AC). With a pinned RNG seed
  the engine must reproduce these exact numbers:

  - *Initiative.* Dmitri: Dex 4 + Intuition 1 = +5; rolls 7 → *12*. Bobbin:
    Intuition 2 + Dex 1 = +3; rolls 10 → *13*. *Bobbin acts first.*
  - *Bobbin's turn.* Bobbin *Charges* (Combine Stride + Strike, spending 1
    Stamina) at Dmitri with a longsword.
  - *Dmitri's reaction.* Dmitri *Dodges*, rolling a Mobility check of *18*.
  - *Resolution.* Bobbin's attack total is *17*. It beats Dmitri's Armor Class
    of *15* (would hit) but the Dodge substitutes the Mobility result 18 for
    Dmitri's AC, and 17 < 18 — *the attack misses* ("falls on empty air").
  - *Budget after:* each has spent 1 action this round; Bobbin has spent 1
    Stamina. Dmitri then Steps as part of resolving Dodge.

  (Note: 17 does not exceed 18, so no hit and no crit. Dmitri's AC 15 is
  consistent with Leather +1 + Dex 4 under this spec's armor table.)

  These are the calls AEF made to make the spec self-consistent and complete.

  1. *Armor table* (§Armor) — authored AC bases and Light/Medium/Heavy

  3. *Spell-attack bonus* — *+0* in v0: spell attack = d20 + spellcasting
     attribute, with no separate proficiency term (the source's sample spells
     add the casting attribute only to damage, and give no spell-proficiency
     analog to weapon Prof). Kept as a named, data-driven field so you can
     raise it later (e.g. +2 for martial parity). *This is the least-certain
     decision — flag if you want a nonzero value.* *Author* - Approved. May change as balance is adjusted, but the vision is that for most characters non-armor defenses will be lower than armor, so spells are more accurate for targeting these.
  4. *Skill model* — flat d20 + governing attribute; trained = +2; only
     Mobility and Determination are combat-critical. Ranked skills deferred. *Author* - Approved.
  5. *Condition magnitudes* — Haste +2 actions, Stunned −2 actions; Take Cover
     +2 to AC/Reflex; Opportunistic Strike triggers on an adjacent enemy
