#+TITLE: CR-001 — Action Economy: per-ROUND budget, not per-turn
#+DATE: 2026-06-30
#+AUTHOR: Guy Verisage
#+TYPE: change-request
#+STATUS: OPEN
#+SPEC: docs/hollow-world-v0-spec.org (APPROVED v0-release-1) — § round economy
#+SOURCE_OF_TRUTH: docs/design-source/Combat.pdf

* Objective (the ONLY objective of this iteration)

  Correct the combat action economy so the seven-action budget is spent
  *per ROUND* (shared across a combatant's turns AND their reactions), not
  refilled *per turn*. This is the single, complete scope of this change-request
  iteration. See "Out of scope" — everything else is COMPLETE-BY-OMISSION and is
  NOT a remediation target for V&V.

* Defect

  The approved v0 spec (=docs/hollow-world-v0-spec.org=, § round economy) is
  explicit and even names this defect ("the misreading recorded in CR-001"):

  - A combatant receives *seven actions at the START of each ROUND* — a
    *per-round* budget, not a per-turn one.
  - Within a round, combatants take turns in initiative order and the order
    *cycles repeatedly*. On each turn a combatant takes *exactly one action*
    (cost 1 from the round budget) plus an optional free Step.
  - *Reactions* (Block/Parry/Dodge/Opportunistic Strike/Shield/Steel) spend one
    action from the *same* 7-action round budget, taken out of turn.
  - A combatant with *0 actions remaining is Spent*: no further turns, actions,
    or reactions this round.
  - The *round ends* when EVERY combatant is Spent; budgets then reset to 7.

  The current code models this as a per-turn budget:

  - =core/rules/action_economy.gd= — both =start_of_turn()= and =end_of_round()=
    refill =_budget = MAX_BUDGET=. The =start_of_turn()= refill is the bug: it
    hands a combatant a fresh 7 every turn and wipes any reaction spend.
  - =core/combat/encounter.gd= — =run_round()= visits each combatant once and
    lets them Strike up to 7 times back-to-back ("act until Spent"), then resets
    everyone. This bakes the per-turn model into the round loop: the
    highest-initiative combatant can fire all 7 Strikes before any opponent acts.

* Required behaviour (acceptance criteria)

  - AC-1: =start_of_turn()= clears only the per-TURN flag(s) (=_movement_used=);
    it does NOT refill =_budget= and does NOT clear =_is_spent=. A combatant who
    became Spent earlier in the round stays Spent until round end.
  - AC-2: The 7-action budget resets to =MAX_BUDGET= only at the ROUND boundary
    (=end_of_round()=, or an explicit =start_of_round()=). =_dazed= still persists
    across rounds (cleared only via =set_dazed(false)=).
  - AC-3: =spend_action()= and =spend_reaction()= both draw down the same
    =_budget=; a reaction taken on another combatant's turn reduces the actions
    available on the reacting combatant's own subsequent turns that round.
  - AC-4: =encounter.gd run_round()= executes a round as repeated cycles through
    =_turn_order= in initiative order, each living non-Spent combatant taking
    *exactly one* action per cycle, until all living combatants are Spent or the
    outcome is decided. Strikes INTERLEAVE in initiative order.
  - AC-5: All existing behaviour is otherwise preserved: initiative tagging, AI
    delegation for enemies, party Strike targeting, damage application,
    =_round_events= emission (attack / combatant_defeated / encounter_result),
    =_round_history= records including =budget_remaining=, and win/loss detection.
  - AC-6: File/inline docs that say "per turn"/"per combatant turn" are corrected
    to "per round" so documentation matches behaviour.

* Expected side effect — balance shifts (do NOT tune)

  Because Strikes now interleave in initiative order instead of one side firing
  all 7 first, the balance simulator win-rate WILL change. This is correct.
  Do NOT edit =core/sim/simulator.gd=, character/.tres stats, or damage dice to
  hit any target win-rate as part of this CR. If the N=1000 balance gate falls
  outside [0.40, 0.80] after the fix, RECORD the new number and flag it for a
  separate balance-tuning decision — it is out of scope here.

  - The d6→d12 longsword balance re-add and any balance tuning.
  - Any new feature, refactor, condition, spell, or subsystem not listed under
