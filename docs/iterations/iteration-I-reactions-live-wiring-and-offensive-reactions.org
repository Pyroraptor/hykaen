* PART B — P1: Enemy reaction AI policy

Give the enemy a real defensive brain on the MainScene.gd:1008 (player-strikes-enemy) path.

** B-1: A deterministic enemy reaction policy implementing ReactionChooserPort
- NEW infrastructure/-layer chooser (e.g. infrastructure/ai_reaction_policy.gd) implementing
  core/application/ports/reaction_chooser_port.gd `choose(legal_reactions, context) -> String`,
  interchangeable with AutoReactionChooser but with a NON-trivial policy. It is NOT the "pass"
  double — do not reuse AutoReactionChooser for live enemy play.
- POLICY (✓ CONFIRMED by Guy 2026-07-08 — "good heuristic, make it more sophisticated later"):
  "react if it helps and is affordable, else Pass." Deterministic and seed-stable (no RNG).
  Precedence given the incoming attack context: if the hit would deal enough damage to matter and
  a legal reaction meaningfully mitigates it, take the single best available legal reaction (Parry
  if it can beat the roll > Block for hard hits > Dodge to negate), else Pass. Budget-aware: never
  react below the reserve the enemy needs for its own next turn — RESERVE = keep ≥1 action. The
  run MUST surface the chosen damage/mitigation thresholds in the design output (not bury them),
  but this simple heuristic is the accepted target for THIS sprint; sophistication is deferred.
- WIRE it to the player-strikes-enemy path: at MainScene.gd:1008, before resolve(), open the
  ENEMY's window resolved_by "enemy" through this policy (synchronous choose(); NO pop-up for
  the enemy) and pass the chosen defense in — the mirror of A-3, using the AI chooser instead
  of the player pop-up. Enemy reaction resolution reuses the SAME resolution.gd path.

** B — acceptance
- Live: when Titus Strikes the Skeleton, the Skeleton sometimes Dodges/Blocks/Parries (no
  pop-up), visibly spends a reaction (its budget drops — the enemy-budget display fix already
  shows this), and does so deterministically for a given seed. When it can't help or can't
  afford it, it Passes. Balance sim stays deterministic (sim path still PASSes, B does not
  touch round_session.gd:545).

* PART C — P2: Opportunistic Strike (the offensive reaction)

Trigger: an ADJACENT hostile Strides. Effect: the reactor may spend a reaction to make a melee
Strike against the mover BEFORE the Stride resolves. Melee-only via the relaxable predicate
already in reaction_window.gd:353 (future talents may relax it — do NOT hardcode past it).

** C-1: TRIGGER emission on the mover's Stride path (both movers)
- ✓ SYMMETRIC (CONFIRMED by Guy 2026-07-08): BOTH the player's Stride AND the enemy's Stride
  provoke Opportunistic Strikes from eligible adjacent hostiles. Not a drop candidate.
- The Stride commit points are: ai.gd's stride (enemy mover — decide_action at ai.gd:189 already
  EXECUTES the Stride inside the decision; the opportunistic window must open BEFORE that Stride
  mutates position) and the player Stride (MainScene _verb_bar_stride_execute → movement path).
- Before a Stride resolves, for each OTHER combatant that is (a) adjacent to the mover's START
  hex, (b) hostile, (c) has a reaction available (not Spent), and (d) has melee reach, build a
  trigger {trigger_type:"OpportunisticStrike", attack_context:{melee_reach:true, weapon:<reactor
  weapon>}, reactor_action_economy:<reactor AE>, ...} and open its window. reaction_window.gd
  ALREADY returns [opportunistic_strike] as the legal reaction for this trigger — do NOT rebuild
  the legality.
- Reactor identity ⇒ chooser: reactor is the PLAYER ⇒ pop-up (player_reaction_chooser); reactor
  is an ENEMY ⇒ the Part B AI policy decides (if Part B is dropped, the enemy never opportunistic-
  strikes — record that coupling in the deferred doc).

** C-2: EFFECT resolution — a reactive melee Strike BEFORE the move
- On an opportunistic_strike choice, resume() (reaction_window.gd:151) TODAY only spends the
  reaction + emits ReactionResolvedEvent — it does NOT resolve the reactive attack. Part C must
  resolve a melee Strike from the reactor against the MOVER, through the SAME consolidated
  resolution path Part A established (resolve_action_use_case.resolve() / resolve_weapon_attack),
  and it must land BEFORE the mover's Stride commits (ordering matters: the strike can down the
  mover, which cancels the Stride). Pay via spend_reaction (already how resume() spends) — NOT
  spend_action; a reactor that already acted this round may still opportunistic-strike if not
  Spent.
- ✓ ORDERING & interrupt semantics (CONFIRMED by Guy 2026-07-08): resolve the opportunistic
  Strike, then — if the mover survives and is still adjacent-eligible / the Stride is still legal —
  complete the Stride; if the mover is DOWNED, CANCEL the Stride. Multiple eligible reactors:
  resolve in INITIATIVE ORDER.

** C — acceptance
- Live: when the Skeleton Strides out of / through Titus's melee reach, Titus gets an
  Opportunistic Strike pop-up; taking it resolves a Strike on the Skeleton BEFORE it moves and
  spends Titus's reaction. Symmetric for the enemy reactor via the Part B policy. Melee-only
  (a ranged-only reactor is never offered it).
