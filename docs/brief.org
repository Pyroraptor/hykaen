* Purpose

Build a short, shippable, turn-based narrative CRPG set in the world
of *Hykaen* (continent of *Sear-Ulf*), running on the author's custom
tabletop ruleset, the *Hollow World* system, implemented in *Godot 4 /
GDScript*.

The first release is a tightly-scoped *vignette*, set on the *Old
Continent of Grok before the Demonstorms*: a four-person adventuring
party discovers evidence of the coming invasion deep within an
ignominious dungeon and swears an oath to one another to counter it.
That party is the *origin story* of two figures the present-day lore
already names — *Titus*, the Human Fighter who will become the *First
Duke of Ulfendon*, and *Gram Marx*, the Tiefling Wizard who will later
research how to end the Storms and break the land bridge. This vignette
ships as a standalone short game *and* becomes the combat-tutorial /
prologue for the eventual full campaign (a present-day party racing to
stop the *second* invasion).

This project serves three goals simultaneously:
1. *A real game to ship* — a complete, polished vignette.
2. *An AEF dogfood* — a greenfield, Godot-based build that stress-tests
   AEF outside its established web + Expo/RN stack.
3. *A portfolio piece* — an end-to-end artifact demonstrating what AEF
   can produce from a brief to a cross-platform binary.

* Problem Statement

Two distinct problems must be solved together.

** Design problem: the ruleset is still in flux

The Hollow World system is partially specified across seven source
documents. It is internally rich but contains gaps and contradictions
(see =Open Questions= below). The most consequential:

- The *"New Subraces"* content is still written in raw D&D 5e terms
  (Charisma, Wisdom, Intelligence, proficiency bonus, bonus actions,
  cantrips, 5e-style ASIs). *Decision (author, 2026-06-22): this is a
  leftover artifact from running 5e games in the setting and is CUT.*
  Racial mechanics are out of scope for v1 (the party is hand-authored;
  races are cosmetic in the vignette).
- The *Skills* section is an empty stub (=Building a Character.pdf=
  literally reads "Skills / Skills"); skill-based exploration and
  social encounters depend on it.
- The eight classes are each specified to the *same* depth (Class HP,
  Stamina/Skills formulas, spellcasting attribute, a list of talent
  *trees*) but the *contents* of the talent trees and the full spell
  lists do not exist in the source docs — only a handful of sample
  spells and one named talent (Lethality) are given.
- Some *derived stats had no formula in the source at all*. The author
  has since supplied the *HP* and *defense* formulas (see the ruleset
  digest), but the following remain undefined and must be authored for
  v0: an *armor list/table* (no =Armor.pdf= exists), character *speed*,
  and the precise *spell-attack* bonus. These are hard gaps v0 fills,
  not contradictions to resolve.
- =Races of Hykaen.pdf= leaves *Humans*, *Tieflings*, and *Goblins* as
  empty stubs, and the "New Subraces" block is the 5e-termed artifact
  noted above.
- The *seven-actions-per-round* economy is unusually large and
  unproven; it is the system's signature mechanic and its biggest
  balance risk.

A custom combat system is the highest-value, highest-uncertainty
component of the game. Building game code on an unstable spec produces
churn. *Therefore the first deliverable is a frozen, self-consistent
v0 ruleset spec scoped to exactly what the vignette needs* — not the
whole system.

** Engineering problem: AEF + Godot is unproven

AEF's playbooks to date target web (=aef-web-client=) and Expo/React
Native (EQTRACKER, HAULNDIRT). Godot introduces new questions the
brief must answer up front:

- *Headless execution in CI*: =godot --headless= must run the test
  suite (GUT) and exit non-zero on failure, with no display server.
- *Agent-editable formats*: =.gd= is plain text; =.tscn= / =.tres=
  use Godot's text resource format (good for diffs and agent edits).
  Binary =.scn=/=.res= are to be avoided in source.
- *Verification without a human watching the screen*: how does AEF
  confirm combat behaves correctly? (Answer: a headless, deterministic
  rules engine with unit tests, plus a simulation harness — see
  Architecture.)
- *Asset pipeline*: programmer art / placeholder assets generated or
  sourced so AEF is never blocked on art it cannot produce.

* World & Story Canon (consolidated reference)

Condensed from =Geography of Hykaen=, =Gods of Hykaen=, =Races of
Hykaen=. This section is the canonical lore reference AEF builds
against; resolve any conflict in favor of this digest (and flag it).

** The shape of the world

- Two continents: the *Lost Continent of Grok* (former heartland of
  mortal civilization) and *Sear-Ulf* (the colony continent, the
  game's setting), nearest each other at the poles.
- The *Stormseas* / *Storm Years*: cyclical, decade-long periods when
  the oceans become impassable, severing the colonies from their
  mother nations.
- *The Demonstorms* (the first invasion): an Abyssal horde — surging
  Gnoll/Orc populations, greater Demons, whole cities vanishing
  overnight — consumed Grok during a Storm Year. Only *Mansear*, which
  controlled the polar *land bridge*, kept contact with the homeland
  (Jerisear) and learned of it. Two fortresses guarded the bridge:
  *Ripliesser* on the Old-Continent side (whose defenders died
  covering the last refugees) and *Riplianckh* on the Sear-Ulf side
  (which held). The South ignored the warnings until the *Duke of
  Ulfendon* marched north; with the magic of his wizard *Gram Marx*,
  the Sear halted the horde and *destroyed the land bridge*, severing
  the Abyss's path. In gratitude, the surviving Searan Houses each
  pledged an elite warrior, scout, arcanist, artist, or scholar to the
  Duke — *fourteen* champions in all, who then served in his court.
- "From Sear to Ulf" → *Sear-Ulf*: the Duke went on to unite the
  South; the phrase for "a great distance" became the continent's name.
- *Present day* (~400 years after the conquest — pinned: =200= years
  from the conquest to the First Duke's line dying out, then another
  =200= under the Steward): the realm fractured, a near-powerless
  *Steward of the Throne* rules only the city of *Ulf*, and fear,
  xenophobia, and cults (including daemoniacs) are rising — the omens of
  a *second* invasion that the full game addresses. *The vignette
  predates all of this*: it is set on the Old Continent before the
  Demonstorms and follows the young Titus and Gram Marx — the future
  First Duke and his wizard — long before the march north (see the
  /Vignette narrative/ below).

** Regions (for map/setting work)

- *North (Mansear)*: Hroww mountains (Frost Giants), Erester River
  basin (heavily wooded, capital *Searan* on a fey spring), Lengfell
  tundra, the fortress *Riplianckh* at the old land bridge.
- *Paghfirld Wildlands*: central steppes/forests/deserts; free goblin
  tribes; poorly mapped.
- *South*: Ulfin Highlands (city of *Ulf*, three-tiered), Inmou high
  desert, Marengian Lowlands (breadbasket, chivalric lords), Korabath
  (rocky seafarers, Drakoran/dragonborn roots), the Sulesse
  (jungles/savannah city-states).

** Peoples & gods (flavor anchors)

- Races: Dwarves (Broken Anvil refugees; Mountain/Hill), Elves
  (Mansear; Winter Eladrin, High, Wood, Wild/Errentei, City),
  Dragonborn (Korabath; Khadzish tongue), Gnomes (nomads),
  Half-Elves, Halflings, Humans, plus rarer Goblinoids, Tieflings,
  Half-Orcs. Note: the elven *Dark Elf* myth ties to the destruction
  of the Feywild — a useful demon/shadow thread.
- A deliberately *secular* pantheon of distant, alien gods worshipped
  through *avatars* (gods rarely intervene; nonbelievers are common).
  Twelve are named in =Gods of Hykaen=. Antagonist anchor: *Ghierdeus,
  Dark Lord of Flames* (Chaotic Evil; Fire, Destruction; an insectoid
  mandible with six blinded eyes) — plus violent *daemoniac* cults, the
  natural divine threads for the demonic invasion. Plausible patrons for
  a Divine Channeler PC: *Roth, the Lord of Law* (LG; Justice, War,
  Protection), *Madara the Hearthkeeper* (LG; Fire, Hospitality), or
  *Hamerung the Merciful* (NG; Mercy, Sun, Life).

** Vignette narrative (author-specified)

The vignette is set on the *Old Continent of Grok, before the
Demonstorms*. A four-person adventuring party delves an ignominious
dungeon and, deep within it, discovers concrete evidence of the coming
Abyssal invasion. Across the delve they confirm the threat and, at the
finale, swear an oath to one another to counter it. This is the
*founding oath* the present-day campaign's lore looks back on — and the
origin of the First Duke and his wizard. The party:

- *Titus* — Human *Fighter*. In the years between the vignette and the
  Demonstorms he becomes the *Duke of Ulfendon*, and fulfils his oath by
  uniting the South and raising the army that marches north.
- *Gram Marx* — Tiefling *Wizard*. He researches how to end the
  Stormseas; in the established canon, his magic helps the Sear halt the
  horde and *destroy the land bridge*.
- *Marigold* — Elven *Divine Warrior* on the *Divine Champion* talent
  path, built for *stealth and Dexterity* (filling the skirmisher role a
  Rogue otherwise would). She stays at Titus' side thereafter.
- *Patience Stoneshield* — Dwarf *Divine Channeler*.

The party is *hand-authored*, so Human / Tiefling / Elf / Dwarf are
purely cosmetic here — no racial mechanics are required (consistent with
the race-cut decision above). This narrative supersedes the earlier
placeholder anchor and resolves Open Question #6.

* Hollow World ruleset digest (for the rules engine)

Captured from =Combat.pdf=, =Building a Character.pdf=, =Classes.pdf=,
=Magic and Spellcasting.pdf=. This is the v0 implementation target.

** Attributes (seven)

Strength, Constitution, Dexterity, Willpower, Intellect, Intuition,
Social. Point-buy from 0; 27 points; cost table:

| Score | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 |  6 |
|-------+---+---+---+---+---+----|
| Cost  | 1 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 9 | 12 |

** Derived stats

- *Hit Points* (formula author-specified): =HP = Race HP + Class HP ×
  Level + Constitution × Tier=. The source's flat per-class *Class HP*
  values are Fighter 6, Divine Warrior 6, Primal Warrior 7, Primal
  Channeler 6, Rogue 5, Divine Channeler 5, Wizard 4, Sorcerer 4. For
  the vignette, *Tier = 1* and *Level = 1*, and *Race HP is normalized
  to 8* (it spans 6–10 across races, but the party is hand-authored and
  races are cosmetic). So a v0 character's HP = =8 + Class HP +
  Constitution= — e.g. Titus the Fighter has 14 + Con, Gram Marx the
  Wizard has 12 + Con. Race-specific Race HP and Tier/Level scaling are
  deferred past v0.
- *Stamina*: =base + Willpower + Constitution=, base per class (Fighter
  4, Sorcerer 4, Divine Warrior 3, Rogue/Wizard*/Divine Channeler/Primal
  Channeler/Primal Warrior 2; Wizard base is 1). Shared resource spent
  on healing and on *Combining Actions* (1 Stamina per combine); fully
  refills only on an uninterrupted long rest.
- *Defenses* (formula author-specified): each is =10 + base
  armor/defense + attributes=, specifically —
  - *Armor Class* = =10 + armor + Dexterity= (Dex applies only in light
    or no armor);
  - *Reflex* = =10 + Dexterity + Intuition=;
  - *Fortitude* = =10 + Constitution + Strength=;
  - *Will* = =10 + Willpower + Social=.
  Balance is *untested* — this is a deliberate starting point. Still
  outstanding: an *armor table* (analogous to the weapon table) giving
  each armor's AC base and its light/medium/heavy category, which v0
  must author.
- *Initiative*: =d20 + Intuition + Dexterity= (confirmed by the
  Bobbin/Dmitri worked example).
- *Attack rolls*: =d20 + (Strength or Dexterity) + weapon Proficiency
  bonus= vs the target defense (the weapon table carries a per-weapon
  Prof. bonus of +1 to +3). Spell attacks are =d20 + spellcasting
  attribute (+ TBD)= — the spell-attack bonus is not fully pinned and
  is a v0 decision.
- *Speed*: never stated; v0 must set a default (suggest 30 ft / 6
  squares). Movement is in 5-ft squares/hexes.
- *Skills known*: =base + Intellect= (Rogue 4, Wizard/Divine Channeler/
  Sorcerer/Primal 2, Fighter/Divine Warrior 1). The skill *list itself
  is an empty stub* — see Open Questions.

** The round (combat core)

- A round ≈ 6 seconds. Each participant gets *seven actions* per
  round (modifiable by effects like Haste / Stunned).
- Initiative once at combat start sets turn order.
- On a turn a participant may *Step* (a free 5-ft move that triggers no
  reactions — distinct from the *Stride* action, which moves up to your
  speed and can be reacted to) and take *one action*. Taking the free
  Step forbids your action that turn from having the Movement trait.
- A participant with no actions left is *spent* (cannot act/react).
- *Reactions* cost one of your remaining actions and trigger off events;
  a spent character cannot react.
- *Critical hit*: attack exceeds the targeted defense by *10+* → roll
  damage dice twice and double the modifier (talents can lower the
  threshold / add dice).
- *Damage scaling by Tier*: =1[W]= → =2[W]= (T2) → =3[W]= (T3), where
  =[W]= is the weapon die.

*** Actions / reactions to implement (v0 set)

These are the *canonical* names and the full set from =Combat.pdf=
(the brief previously listed several non-existent actions — Dash,
Lunge, Charge — which do not exist as discrete actions):

- Actions: *Strike, Stride, Cast a spell, Interact, Guard, Take Cover*,
  plus *Step* (a free/"Special" 5-ft move).
- Reactions: *Block, Parry, Dodge, Opportunistic Strike, Steel,
  Shield* (a spell).
- *Combinations* (not separate actions): spend *1 Stamina* to fuse two
  actions into one — e.g. *Charge* = Stride+Strike, *Riposte* =
  Block/Parry + Strike, sprint = Stride+Stride, double-attack =
  Strike+Strike. Only actions with the *Repeat* trait can be fused with
  themselves. The *Dazed* condition forbids all combining.

Defenses split into *passive* (AC/Reflex/Fortitude/Will, always-on
target numbers) and *active* reactions that substitute a check or
reduce/negate: *Dodge* (Mobility check vs AC/Reflex; needs an open
square to Step into and you must not be Immobilized), *Block* (reduce
damage by 1[W]+weapon Block value; only vs AC), *Parry* (contested
attack roll + weapon Parry bonus; only vs AC), and *Steel*
(Determination check vs Fortitude/Will; conscious only). This
active+passive duality is the system's defining tactical hook and must
be modeled as a clean, testable resolution pipeline.

** Spellcasting (three paradigms)

- *Channeling* (Divine + Primal classes — Divine Channeler, Divine
  Warrior, Primal Channeler, Primal Warrior): a per-round energy pool
  (max = spellcasting attribute) filled by the =Channel= action, spent
  on spells; empties at end of round.
- *Prepared/attuned* (Wizard and other Magi, e.g. Swordmage): prepare
  known spells into attuned items (count = attuned items; total energy
  ≤ level); an item discharges on cast and must be re-primed (0-energy
  spells don't discharge). Wizards can also ritual-cast.
- *Sorcerer*: converts *Stamina* directly into spell energy as part of
  the casting action.
- Spells carry: action cost, energy cost, components (Somatic, Verbal,
  Material, Implement) and damage keywords (Fire, Life, …), hands
  required, Concentration, range, damage/effect. Spellcasting attribute
  by class: *Wizard = Intellect, Divine Channeler = Social, Divine
  Warrior = Willpower, Sorcerer = Willpower, Primal Channeler/Warrior =
  Intuition*. (Sample spells in the source: Channel, Shield, Fire Bolt,
  Heal, Fireball.)

** Classes (vignette subset in bold)

The source defines *eight* classes on a =<Source> <Channeler|Warrior>=
naming pattern, grouped by power source:

- Martial: *Fighter*, Rogue (Spellcasting N/A)
- Arcane: *Wizard* (Intellect), Sorcerer (Willpower)
- Divine: *Divine Channeler* (Social — this is the source's "Cleric"),
  *Divine Warrior* (Willpower — the source's "Holy Warrior")
- Primal: Primal Channeler (Intuition), Primal Warrior (Intuition)

*Naming note:* "Cleric", "Druid", "Warden", "Paladin" appearing in
=Building a Character.pdf= are 4e/flavor references in the attribute
descriptions, *not* class names; the actual classes are the eight
above. *Fighter*, *Wizard*, *Divine Warrior*, and *Divine Channeler*
ship in v1 — the hand-authored vignette party (note this takes the
*Divine Warrior*, Marigold, into the playable set and leaves *Rogue*
deferred). The other four (Rogue, Sorcerer, Primal Channeler, Primal
Warrior) are fully specified at the same depth but deferred. Each class
defines a Class HP value, Stamina/Skills formulas, a spellcasting
attribute (or N/A), and a set of *talent trees*.

** Leveling (Tales of Maj'Eyal-inspired)

Progression is via *talent trees* (e.g. Fighter: Guardian,
Conditioning, Weapon Styles, Tactics…; Rogue: Lethality, Infiltration,
Poisons…). ToME-style: points invested into trees unlock and deepen
talents; some trees gate behind investment levels. v1 implements the
tree/point framework with 1-2 tiers of talents per playable class.

* Proposed Architecture

The central principle: *the rules engine is pure, headless, and
deterministic — fully decoupled from Godot rendering.* This is what
makes the game testable and AEF-verifiable without a human at the
screen.

** Layered structure

#+BEGIN_SRC text
  res://
    core/                 # PURE GDScript — no Node, no rendering
      rules/
        attributes.gd     # 7 attributes, point-buy, derived stats
        action_economy.gd # round/turn/action budget, "spent" state
        resolution.gd     # attack vs defense, active defenses, crits
        damage.gd         # [W] scaling, types, crit doubling
        conditions.gd     # Dazed, Immobilized, Haste, etc.
        rng.gd            # injectable, seedable RNG (determinism)
      model/              # data classes loaded from Resources
        character.gd
        combatant.gd
        spell.gd
        talent.gd
      combat/
        encounter.gd      # combat state machine over core/rules
        ai.gd             # enemy decision-making
      sim/
        simulator.gd      # headless combat runner for tests/balance
    data/                 # CONTENT AS DATA (.tres custom Resources)
      classes/  races/  actions/  spells/  talents/  weapons/
      encounters/  dialogue/  maps/
    game/                 # GODOT layer — Nodes, scenes, presentation
      combat_ui/  exploration/  dialogue_ui/  menus/  save/
    tests/                # GUT tests, headless
      unit/  integration/  sim/
#+END_SRC

** Why this shape

- =core/= has *zero* Godot scene dependencies, so its entire surface is
  unit-testable under =godot --headless= with GUT. Combat correctness
  is asserted in code, not eyeballed.
- *Content is data* (=.tres= custom =Resource= types): actions, spells,
  talents, classes, encounters, and dialogue are authored as resources,
  not hardcoded. This is friendlier for both AEF (small, schema'd,
  diffable edits) and a human designer, and lets balance change without
  touching logic.
- *Deterministic, injectable RNG*: every random outcome flows through a
  seeded =rng.gd=. Tests pin a seed; the simulator replays exact
  combats; bugs are reproducible.
- =sim/= lets AEF run *thousands of headless combats* to check balance
  invariants (e.g. "Tier 1 party wins encounter 3 between 55-75% of
  the time", "no encounter has zero-variance auto-loss") — automated
  balance feedback no human could provide at that volume.

** Verification strategy (the AEF-critical part)

| Layer            | How AEF verifies it autonomously                      |
|------------------+-------------------------------------------------------|
| Rules engine     | GUT unit tests, headless, seeded RNG, exit code gates |
| Combat flows     | Integration tests driving =encounter.gd= end-to-end   |
| Balance          | =sim/= Monte-Carlo runs asserting win-rate invariants |
| UI / scenes      | Headless render-to-image screenshots + visual diff    |
| Full vignette    | Scripted playthrough harness hitting each story beat  |
| Cross-platform   | Export-template CI builds per platform; smoke-launch   |

* Implementation Plan (phased)

** Phase 0 — Freeze the ruleset v0 (gating, human-in-loop)

Resolve the contradictions in =Open Questions= for *only* the vignette
subset. Produce =docs/hollow-world-v0-spec.org= as the single source
of truth: the seven attributes, the action economy, the v0 action/
reaction/spell/talent cards, the four playable-adjacent classes, and
HP/Stamina/defense formulas. Port any 5e-termed content into Hollow
World terms or cut it from v1.

Deliverable: a frozen, self-consistent spec. *AEF does not build combat
logic before this exists.*

** Phase 1 — Rules engine core (headless, no scenes)

Implement =core/= against the v0 spec: attributes/derived stats, the
round/action-economy state machine, attack-vs-defense resolution with
active defenses, crit rule, damage/Tier scaling, a starter set of
conditions, and the seeded RNG. Comprehensive GUT unit tests.

Deliverable: =godot --headless= runs a green test suite that exercises
the full combat resolution pipeline. No rendering yet.

** Phase 2 — Playable combat in Godot

Build the =game/combat_ui/=: grid/positioning, turn & action-budget
HUD, action selection, reaction prompts, target/defense display,
damage feedback. Wire one full encounter end-to-end (player party vs
enemies, win/lose). Enemy =ai.gd= v1.

Deliverable: a human can play one complete combat encounter; the
scripted playthrough harness drives it headlessly.

** Phase 3 — Characters, classes, talents, content pipeline

The =.tres= authoring pipeline for classes/actions/spells/talents/
weapons; the talent-tree framework with 1-2 tiers per playable class;
the pre-generated party; the spell paradigms needed by the party's
classes. Add the =sim/= balance harness.

Deliverable: the fixed party is fully realized in data; balance sims
run in CI.

** Phase 4 — Exploration & narrative

Small tile/grid maps, interaction, a minimal skill-check system (the
exploration/social half), a branching *dialogue system* with flags and
2-3 choice points, and save/checkpoint.

Deliverable: the player can move between scenes, talk, make choices,
and trigger combats; state persists.

** Phase 5 — Vignette build, polish, export

Author the actual vignette content (story beats, the 3-5 encounters,
the set-piece, the oath finale), placeholder art/audio, main menu,
and cross-platform export templates in CI.

Deliverable: a downloadable, playable vignette on desktop (web export
as stretch).

* Key Design Decisions

** Engine: Godot 4 / GDScript

Chosen (by the author). Reinforced by AEF-fit: =.gd= and =.tscn=/=.tres=
are text and diff cleanly; =godot --headless= + GUT gives real CI;
true cross-platform export from one codebase. *Decision: keep all
source resources in text format (=.tres=/=.tscn=), never binary, so
agents and version control can read every change.*

** Rules engine decoupled from rendering

Non-negotiable. It is the only way to make combat correctness
machine-verifiable and is the linchpin of the whole AEF-dogfood thesis.

** Content as data, not code

Custom =Resource= types for all game content. Keeps logic stable,
makes authoring an AEF-friendly schema'd-edit task, and lets the human
designer tune without programming.

** Determinism first

Seeded, injected RNG everywhere. Enables reproducible tests, replayable
combats, and the Monte-Carlo balance harness.

** Spec-before-code (Phase 0 gate)

The ruleset is frozen for the vignette subset before combat logic is
written, to stop churn on a moving target.

   names (*Mobility* for Dodge, *Determination* for Steel, plus
   Perception/Stealth/Thievery/Persuasion from the attribute text) — or
   defer skills entirely and gate exploration/social checks on raw
   attributes for v1.
3. *Class roster + naming*: RESOLVED — the v1 roster is the
   hand-authored vignette party: *Fighter* (Titus), *Wizard* (Gram
   Marx), *Divine Warrior* (Marigold, Divine Champion / Dex-stealth
   build), and *Divine Channeler* (Patience Stoneshield, Dwarf). Naming: the source's
   "Cleric" = Divine Channeler, "Holy Warrior" = Divine Warrior. Still
   open: the *contents* of talent trees and full spell lists are absent
   from the source and must be authored for v0 (incl. a stealth-capable
   Divine Champion build for Marigold).
4. *Seven-action economy*: signature but unproven. Phase 1's sim
   harness should pressure-test whether seven actions/round is fun and
   balanced, or wants tuning, *before* heavy content is built on it.
5. *Timeline*: RESOLVED. Present day = conquest + ~200 yrs (Duke's
   line) + ~200 yrs (Steward) ≈ 400 yrs. Dwarven Broken-Anvil migration
   ≈ 700 yrs ago (predates the conquest). *The vignette sits at the
   Demonstorms / Duke's northern march*, ~400 yrs before the full
   game's present.
6. *Vignette plot*: RESOLVED — see /Vignette narrative/ above. The
   proto-Duke's band (Titus, Gram Marx, Marigold, and Patience
   Stoneshield) discovers the coming Demonstorms in an Old-Continent
   dungeon and swears the founding oath. Remaining detail: the dungeon's
   specifics/encounters.
7. *"Combining Actions" and the Stamina economy*: RESOLVED by
   =Combat.pdf=. Combining two actions into one costs *1 Stamina*;
   Repeat-trait actions may self-combine; reactions may combine with
   actions (riposte); Dazed forbids combining. Stamina is also spent on
   healing (the =Heal= spell trades a target's Stamina for HP) and
   refills only on a long rest.
8. *Derived stats*: PARTIALLY RESOLVED — the four *defense* formulas
   are now author-specified (see Derived stats above; balance untested).
   Still open: the *armor table* (AC base + light/medium/heavy category
   per armor), character *speed* (v0 default suggested 30 ft / 6
   squares), and the precise *spell-attack* bonus. Weapon-attack rolls
   and initiative are derivable from the worked example.
9. *HP model*: RESOLVED — =HP = Race HP + Class HP × Level +
   Constitution × Tier=; for v0, Tier = Level = 1 and Race HP is
   normalized to 8 (see Derived stats above).

* Success Criteria

1. =godot --headless= runs the full GUT suite green in CI; exit codes
   gate merges.
2. The rules engine reproduces, in code, every worked example in
   =Combat.pdf= (e.g. the Bobbin-vs-Dmitri duel) deterministically.
3. A human can play a complete combat encounter with action budget,
   active defenses, reactions, and criticals all functioning.
4. The fixed party, its classes, talents, and spells are defined
   entirely as =.tres= data.
5. The balance harness runs N headless combats and reports win-rate
   invariants per encounter.
6. The full vignette is completable start-to-finish: explore → dialogue
   → choices → combat → the founding-oath finale, with save/checkpoint.
7. A downloadable desktop build exists for at least one platform,
   produced by CI from export templates.
8. No binary scene/resource files in source; every change is a text
   diff.

* Risks

| Risk                                          | Mitigation                                                   |
|-----------------------------------------------+-------------------------------------------------------------|
| Ruleset churn destabilizes combat code        | Phase 0 freeze; build only the v0 subset; spec is law       |
| Seven-action economy isn't fun/balanced       | Sim harness validates balance early, before content scale   |
| AEF unproven on Godot (headless CI, .tscn)    | Phase 1 is headless-only; prove the test loop before scenes |
| Combat unverifiable without a human watching  | Pure decoupled rules engine + seeded RNG + sim harness      |
| Art/audio blocks an agent that can't make it  | Placeholder/programmer art; data-driven so assets swap late |
| Scope creep from a deep world into the full RPG | Hard vignette scope table; everything else is "deferred"   |
| Save/version drift as data schemas evolve     | Versioned save format; data resources carry a schema field  |
